# Contributing to Tesla-Github-Manager

All contributions to this component must strictly adhere to the Vigilum Codex and the absolute orchestration doctrine.

## Doctrine: Producer != Validator (Independent Verification)

The core tenet of our governance model is the separation of generation and validation. 
**The agent or entity that produces the code, plan, or artifact MUST NOT be the same entity that approves its deployment.**

1. **Independent Auditing**: All submissions require an independent audit by a designated gatekeeper (e.g., `tesla-code-auditor` or `tesla-premortem`).
2. **Evidence-Driven Validation**: You must provide a clear evidence chain of successful execution and testing. Extrapolated success is considered a failure.
3. **Impartiality**: Validation must remain factual, objective, and devoid of artificial obstacles or overzealous critique. 

By contributing, you acknowledge and accept these structural constraints. Code without proof will be systematically rejected.
